Journey to Memories

2D animated short film, Western Fantasy x Anime artstyle

01 Overview Quick links and documents 02 Proposal Drafts and animatics 03 Process Making of the work

Abstract

This proposed 2D animation explores loss, guilt, courage, and legacy within a fantasy world threatened by recurring demon raids. The story is about Ann, a young woman who flees her island home during a raid, while a brave warrior remains behind to defend the city. After the raid, Ann returned to the island and searched for the man, but later found him dead. Overcome by grief and guilt, she reached his grave and reflected on their shared memories. Her mourning becomes a turning point as she accepts his insignia and inherits his sense of duty. Years later, Ann emerges as a powerful protector who saves a child from danger, carrying on the warrior's legacy. The animation would be in a Japanese anime-inspired art style with a Western fantasy setting.
